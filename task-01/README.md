# 🏴‍☠️ One Piece Terminal Adventure

## Prologue – The Logbook of the Grand Line

> _"A pirate who relies only on memory will never reach the end of the Grand Line."_

Welcome, Captain.

You are about to embark on a voyage unlike any other. The seas ahead are filled with hidden truths, forgotten histories, and mysteries buried beneath layers of deception. Every island you visit will test not only your courage, but also your ability to observe, investigate, and think like a true explorer.

Rumors speak of a legendary Devil Fruit known only as the **Gito Gito no Mi**. Unlike any ordinary Devil Fruit, its power does not command fire, storms, or the sea.

It commands **history**.

Those who awaken its abilities can uncover forgotten events, walk through parallel timelines, recover what has been erased, and reshape the course of history itself.

The World Government has spent years trying to suppress its existence.

Your journey begins where many great pirate legends began...

**Loguetown.**

## 📖 Before You Set Sail

A true pirate always keeps a captain's log.

Before starting, open **another terminal window or tab** and dedicate it as your **Logbook**. Throughout your adventure you will recover flags, cipher fragments, transmission codes, and other pieces of information.
Some discoveries are only meaningful when combined with others.

## A wise captain records everything exactly as it was found.

# LEVEL 1 — AWAKENING AT LOGUETOWN REEF

_"Every great voyage begins with a single discovery."_

The waters surrounding Loguetown are unusually quiet.

Hidden somewhere along the reef is the legendary **Gito Gito no Mi**, a Devil Fruit feared even by the World Government. Before anyone could claim it, Smoker and his Marines seized the shipment and divided it among four secure storage sectors.

But the Marines are cautious.

Knowing pirates would come searching, they filled the warehouses with countless perfect replicas. At first glance, every crate appears identical.

Yet no imitation can truly copy freedom.

Marine property is manufactured, catalogued, and permanently sealed under strict control.

A true Devil Fruit refuses to be owned.

Somewhere among the cargo lies an object that still possesses the freedom to change itself.

Only by eating the genuine fruit will its true power awaken. (Use ./eat.sh <file_name>)

May your journey begin.

# LEVEL 2 — THE TWO FACES OF WHISKEY PEAK

_"Not every island tells the same story."_

Your newly awakened power carries you safely into the Grand Line.

The people of Whiskey Peak celebrate your arrival with music, food, and laughter. Yet something feels... wrong.

While the crew enjoys the festivities, you notice Zoro watching the townsfolk with unusual suspicion.

Beneath the welcoming smiles exists another version of this island—a hidden history concealed beside the one everyone sees.

The communications vault recognizes the aura of the awakened Gito Gito no Mi.

The intercepted report reveals more than the truth behind Whiskey Peak.

Hidden within the transmission is an Executive Transmission Code used by high-ranking Baroque Works officers.

Ordinary agents memorize these codes.

Experienced pirates write them down.

Keep it safe.

## The code may not appear in this form again.

# LEVEL 3 — THE WAX LABYRINTH OF LITTLE GARDEN

_"The easiest way to hide something is to bury it beneath everything else."_

Little Garden should have been an island of giants.

Instead, it has become a labyrinth of wax.

Mr. 3 has transformed the jungle into an impossible maze where every path leads to another dead end and every crate looks identical to the last.

Hundreds of intercepted Baroque Works transmissions lie scattered throughout the forest.

Every report appears authentic.

Every report bears the seal of an officer.

Yet only one was ever transmitted by Baroque Works Executive Command.

Fortunately, you recovered the Executive Transmission Code while investigating Whiskey Peak.

There is one problem.

Executive Codes are never transmitted in their original form. Before being sent across the Den Den Mushi network, every transmission identifier is converted into its broadcast representation.

Somewhere inside this labyrinth, one intercepted report still carries that transformed identifier.

Find the genuine report.

Within it lies the first cipher fragment needed to uncover the road ahead..

# LEVEL 4 — THE CAMOUFLAGED BLUEPRINTS OF WATER 7

_"Names can be changed. True nature cannot."_

The city of Water 7 stands on edge.

CP9 agents have infiltrated Galley-La Company in search of the legendary blueprints of the Sea Train, **Puffing Tom**.

Knowing they would search every document by name, Iceburg devised a simple but brilliant deception.

He stripped the blueprint of its identity.

What remains no longer looks like a blueprint at all.

Those who judge by appearances will see only meaningless corruption.

But every object remembers what it truly is.

Ask it not for its name...

Ask it for its nature.

Only then can its true contents be revealed.

Inside awaits the second cipher fragment.

# LEVEL 5 — THE BUSTER CALL TIMELINE RECOVERY

_"History can be destroyed... but it cannot always be erased."_

The Buster Call has arrived.

The Judicial Tower is silent.

Every important document has vanished beneath the bombardment, leaving behind nothing but empty space.

To anyone else, the trail ends here.

But your Devil Fruit grants a power no cannon can destroy.

The past still exists.

Somewhere beyond the present lies a moment before the first cannon fired—a history where the files remain untouched.

Walk backward through time.

Find the last peaceful moment before destruction.

Even the past must recognize who carries the Fruit's awakening.

The surviving records speak of a Poneglyph inscription shattered into two fragments. Neither fragment carries meaning alone. Restore the inscription before attempting to decipher it.
